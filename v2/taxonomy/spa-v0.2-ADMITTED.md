# SPA family module — v0.2 (ADMITTED)

Gate run of 24 July 2026 (Sonnet vs reference, 93 units): level 1 100%,
level 2 97.8%, exact leaf 94.6% — all thresholds passed on first submission.
The module is admitted. Leaf set unchanged from v0.1; four amendments from
the divergence triage.

## Amendments

- **Rule S4 — front matter is not a clause unit.** Parties identification
  blocks, tables of contents and signature blocks are document front matter,
  carried by the document tag (grammar spec v0.1), and are excluded from
  clause tagging. (Resolves I01 and J01, where the reference itself had
  parked front matter in GEN.X; neither label was right because the unit
  should not exist.)
- **Scope note, SPA.4.6/4.8 boundary.** IP infringement and asset-title
  warranties take SPA.4.6; restrictive-covenant and contractual-commitment
  warranties take SPA.4.8; a composite takes its drafting-weight limb.
  (Resolves I22.)
- **Scope note, SPA.4.11.** Reserved for warranties anchored to the
  accounts-date-to-closing window (conduct since the reference date, absence
  of adverse events since). General commercial-relations warranties without
  that temporal anchor take SPA.4.8. (Resolves J25.)
- **Reference correction, J30.** Employment-related specific undertakings
  (plans sociaux, restructuring commitments to employees) reclassified to
  SPA.4.5 — the independent classifier's reading was better than the
  reference. Scope note added to SPA.4.5 accordingly.
