# SPA family module — draft v0.1 (pre-gate)

Status: DRAFT, admission gate pending. Derived from the residual clusters of
the extension stress test (share transfer, 2013, LU) enriched by a second
archive document (cession d'actions, 1999, FR) adding earn-out, closing
mechanics, tiered warranty caps and post-closing covenants. Applies to share
and asset transfer instruments (parts sociales, actions, fonds de commerce);
the family code is SPA. Cross-cutting clauses (GEN, DSP, LIA, TRM, CNF)
keep their core codes inside SPA documents.

## Leaves

**SPA.1 — transfer object.** The operative sale and purchase: quantum,
encumbrance-free transfer, jouissance. Discriminator vs SPA.6: SPA.1 creates
the obligation to transfer; SPA.6 fixes when title and enjoyment pass and
opposability mechanics.

**SPA.2 — price**
- SPA.2.1 fixed price and valuation basis
- SPA.2.2 payment terms: instalments, account, timing
- SPA.2.3 earn-out (complément de prix): conditions, determination,
  expert procedure, acceleration and death/incapacity mechanics

**SPA.3 — closing (réalisation)**
- SPA.3.1 closing deliveries: transfer orders, registers, resignations,
  minutes, counterpart transfers, payment
- SPA.3.2 conditions precedent or resolutory; board approval (agrément)

**SPA.4 — representations and warranties catalogue.** Chapeau clauses take
the parent SPA.4 (hierarchical fallback).
- SPA.4.1 title to the shares: ownership, no encumbrances, no options or
  convertibles, transferability
- SPA.4.2 corporate: existence, solvency, housekeeping and filings,
  registers, bank mandates, participations
- SPA.4.3 accounts: conformity, completeness, provisions, off-balance-sheet
- SPA.4.4 tax and parafiscal compliance
- SPA.4.5 employment and social security
- SPA.4.6 assets: title and encumbrances, movables, fonds de commerce,
  IP, leases and crédit-bail
- SPA.4.7 litigation, regulatory compliance, authorisations
- SPA.4.8 contracts and commitments: change-of-control triggers, brokerage,
  restrictive covenants, specific undertakings
- SPA.4.9 insurance
- SPA.4.10 relations with the seller: current accounts, guarantees given,
  intragroup positions
- SPA.4.11 conduct of business since the accounts date; absence of material
  adverse events
- SPA.4.12 full disclosure and accuracy of information supplied
- SPA.4.X residual (e.g. activity-specific product representations)

**SPA.5 — warranty indemnity (garantie de passif)**
- SPA.5.1 scope: covered losses, loss definition
- SPA.5.2 quantum limits: de minimis trigger, caps, tiered or degressive
  plafonds, proportionality
- SPA.5.3 claims procedure: notification, deadlines, third-party claims and
  defence participation
- SPA.5.4 duration and carve-outs: fiscal and social prescription, title
  unlimited, interruption
- SPA.5.5 knowledge non-exoneration: due diligence and awareness without
  effect on the warranty
- SPA.5.6 security for the warranty: bank guarantee, caution, escrow

**SPA.6 — title transfer and opposability.** Timing of property and
jouissance; opposability to the company and third parties; company
acknowledgement.

**SPA.7 — post-closing covenants**
- SPA.7.1 governance and management undertakings, business plan compliance
- SPA.7.2 seller non-compete and non-solicitation
- SPA.7.3 seller confidentiality post-closing (module leaf; general
  CNF codes remain for standalone NDA-type clauses)

**SPA.8 — costs and expenses.** Allocation of fees, advisers, duties.

## GEN promotions (cross-family, same gate run)

- **GEN.15 — recitals / preamble.** Exposé and context clauses forming part
  of the agreement.
- **GEN.16 — annexes integral.** Clauses making schedules part of the
  agreement.
- **GEN.17 — contract language.** Designation of the authoritative language.
- **Scope note, GEN.3.** Covers transmission to heirs, successors and
  sub-acquirers as well as assignment restrictions.

## Coding rules (module-specific)

- **Rule S1.** R&W sub-heads are classified individually at the smallest
  numbered unit; the catalogue chapeau takes SPA.4.
- **Rule S2.** A clause combining trigger threshold, caps and duration takes
  the limb its drafting weight sits on; pure duration limbs take SPA.5.4.
- **Rule S3.** Seller capacity and authority remain GEN.10 (core); only
  target-related representations enter SPA.4.
